# Your first plant

This lesson is for an owner who has a new sensor stick and the app installed on their phone. In about ten minutes you put the sensor stick into the pot, pair it with the phone and see the first reading of the plant on the screen. The example is a Monstera on a windowsill; use your own plant in its place.

1. Open the app. The first screen shows "Start" and "Join" and asks for no name and no password.
2. Tap "Start". You see the list of plants, still empty, with a control to add a plant.
3. Tap "add". The plant form asks for a name and for lower limits of soil moisture, light and temperature.
4. Type "Monstera" and pick a preset. Do not save yet.
5. Push the sensor stick into the soil near the stem.
6. Tap "put a sensor stick to the plant". The screen tells you to take the cell out and put it back in. If the app asks for the Bluetooth permission, allow it.
7. Take the cell out of the sensor stick and put it back in. The sensor stick is now open for pairing for a few minutes.
8. Tap "Search" and hold the phone close to the pot. Within a few seconds the sensor stick appears, marked "near".
9. Pick it, and accept the dialog of the phone if one appears. You are back in the plant form, which now shows the sensor stick. If the screen says that the sensor stick is no longer open for pairing, repeat steps 7 to 9.
10. Tap "Save". If the app asks whether it may send notifications, answer "Not now". The list of plants shows "Monstera".
11. Wait up to a minute. The list shows the soil moisture with the age of the reading, for example "41 %, a minute ago".
12. Tap the plant. Its screen shows all three quantities and the chart of the history, with one point so far.

The first reading of your plant is on the screen. From now on the phone collects readings by itself; what the app promises on every screen is in [app-ui](../references/app-ui.md).
