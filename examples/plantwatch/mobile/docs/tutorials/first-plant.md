# Your first plant

For an owner who has a new sensor stick and the app installed on their phone. In about ten minutes you put the sensor stick into the pot, pair it with the phone and see the first reading of the plant on the screen. The example is a Monstera on a windowsill; use your own plant in its place.

## 1. Start the app

Open the app. It asks for no name and no password: the first screen offers "Start" and "Join". Tap "Start". You see the list of plants, still empty, with one sentence that says what is missing and a control to add a plant. This list is the home screen; every other screen leads back to it.

## 2. Add the plant

Tap "add". The plant form asks for a name and for lower limits of soil moisture, light and temperature. Type "Monstera" and pick a preset; the limits can be changed later. Do not save yet: the form also offers "put a sensor stick to the plant", and that is the next step.

## 3. Put the sensor stick into the pot and open it for pairing

Push the sensor stick into the soil near the stem. Then tap "put a sensor stick to the plant". The screen tells you to take the cell out of the sensor stick and put it back in: a sensor stick accepts a new phone only for a few minutes after it has started. Do that now. The first time you reach this screen the app asks for the Bluetooth permission and says why; allow it.

## 4. Pair

Tap "Search" and hold the phone close to the pot. Within a few seconds the sensor stick appears, marked "near"; after three seconds without change it is preselected. Pick it. The phone may show a dialog of its own; accept it. When pairing has succeeded you are back in the plant form, which now shows the sensor stick. If the screen says that the sensor stick is no longer open for pairing, take the cell out and in again and search once more.

## 5. Save and see the first reading

Tap "Save". The app asks whether it may send notifications; you can answer "Not now". The list of plants now shows "Monstera". Pairing has started the first fetch at once, and the sensor stick took its first reading a few seconds after its cell went in, so within a minute the list shows the soil moisture of the plant with the age of the reading, for example "41 %, a minute ago". Tap the plant: its screen shows all three quantities and the chart of the history, with one point so far. The time next to the reading comes from the phone's clock; the sensor stick has no clock of its own.

That is the result: the first reading of your plant is on the screen.

## 6. What happens from here

The sensor stick takes a reading every 15 minutes and keeps each one until the phone has fetched it. The phone fetches whenever it comes near the pot, also while the app is closed, as far as the phone's system allows, and every time you open the app near the plant. What it has fetched it shows without a network connection, with the age of the readings on every screen. Whether the plant needs water is not judged on the phone: the readings go to the server with the next network connection, and an alert comes back from there. A plant with an open alert moves to the top of the list with its reason, and if you have allowed notifications you get one message per alert; a tap on it opens the plant.

What the app promises on every screen is in the contract [app-ui](../references/app-ui.md). Why the phone, and not the sensor stick, gives a reading its time is in [reasoning.md](../explanation/reasoning.md).
